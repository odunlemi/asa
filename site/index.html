What is Àṣà?

Àṣà is a real-time English to Yoruba speech-to-speech translation system. It lets you hold a conversation in English and listen to the response in Yoruba — preserving tone, prosody, and the natural rhythm of the language.

Real-time conversations

Tap record, speak in English, and get back spoken Yoruba without typing or switching apps.

Built for mobile

A clean, glass-native React Native interface designed for phones first — with history, audio playback, and status at a glance.

Open models, open code

The backend uses open weights and a FastAPI service; the mobile app and Convex orchestration are open source on GitHub.

How it works

The app records your voice, then a Convex action coordinates transcription, translation, and synthesis through the ML backend.

Step 1

Speak English

Tap the record button in the app and speak naturally. Audio is uploaded to Convex storage.

Step 2

Transcribe

AssemblyAI converts the English audio into text with a signed URL from Convex.

Step 3

Translate

The Davlan/m2m100_418M-eng-yor-mt model translates the English text into Yoruba.

Step 4

Listen

Shinzmann/soro-tts-yor synthesizes Yoruba speech, and the app plays it back.

Stack

Àṣà combines a React Native mobile app, a Convex backend, and a GPU-backed FastAPI service on Modal.
